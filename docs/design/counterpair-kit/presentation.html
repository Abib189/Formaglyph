Selected C — Counterpair

Counterpair

Two independent forms become one recognisable signature.

  • The chosen two-piece asymmetry is preserved; no generic cube, sparkle or AI motif.
  • A restrained title-case wordmark balances the assertive symbol. The F shoulder is redrawn and all letters are outlined.
  • Separate standard, reverse and small-size drawings address weight and counter clarity.
  • Possible numeral-5 reading remains a known risk; build name association rather than inventing a literal story.
